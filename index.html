Reporting Criteria & Instructions

Ensure your observation data meets verification standards prior to submission.

1. Guidelines on How to Fill the Weather Form

To maintain high data quality, follow these instructions when logging your update:

  • Pinpoint Specificity: Provide your exact district, tehsil, or city sector rather than a general broad area.
  • Observation Timing: Note the actual start time and peak time of rainfall or wind gusts, not just submission time.
  • Rainfall Scale: Categorize precipitation carefully (e.g., Light Drizzle, Moderate Continuous, Intense Downpour/Cloudburst).

2. Verification Criteria for Weather Alerts

Submissions flagged as high-severity or emergency hazards require the following criteria:

  • Photo Evidence: Upload clear, unedited visual evidence showing cloud build-up, water levels, or hail size.
  • Device Geotagging: Ensure device location metadata is preserved when taking observation photos.
  • Account Verification: Standard updates can be logged anonymously, but hazard warnings require an authenticated citizen profile.
